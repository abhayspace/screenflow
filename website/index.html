How it works

1 View a screen On the receiving PC, tap “View a screen”, pick Same WiFi or Internet, and get a 6-digit code.
2 Share my screen On the sending PC, pick the screen, choose the same mode, and enter the code.
3 Watch The screen streams peer-to-peer — nothing passes through a server.
