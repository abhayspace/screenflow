About

What is ScreenFlow?

ScreenFlow is a lightweight desktop app for macOS and Windows that lets you cast your screen from one computer to another — on the same WiFi with zero internet, or over the internet with a simple 6-digit code.

How it works

Video streams peer-to-peer over WebRTC. A tiny signaling step pairs the devices; the screen itself never passes through a server.

Who made it

ScreenFlow is built and maintained by nextforms.
