Privacy Policy

What we collect

Screen content: never. Screen video streams peer-to-peer between your devices — it never touches our servers.

Accounts: if you sign up, we store your name, username, and email (in Supabase) — used only for authentication.

Contact/developer messages: sent via Resend to our support inbox.

Signaling

A small relay exchanges connection info (WebRTC offers/answers) when pairing over the internet. It carries no screen data.
